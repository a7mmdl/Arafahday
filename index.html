The promise of this day
مَا مِنْ يَوْمٍ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ أَنْ يُعْتِقَ اللَّهُ فِيهِ عَبْدًا مِنَ النَّارِ مِنْ يَوْمِ عَرَفَةَ
“There is no day on which Allah sets free more people from the Fire than the Day of Arafah.”
The Prophet ﷺ · narrated by ‘Aishah (may Allah be pleased with her) · Sahih Muslim
Action So ask Allah for forgiveness, and to be freed from the Hellfire.

On this day, even as the Prophet ﷺ held his camel’s rein with one hand, he would raise the other to the sky in du’ā.

Because this is the best day of the year for du’ā, and he wanted to make the most of every moment of it.

Ask yourself: what if this were your final year? What would you ask Allah for? How would you spend this golden time?

“Du’ā made on this day can change everything.”

The key window, Dhuhr to Maghrib.  When it comes, do not hold back. Pour out everything.
A way to ask

Begin by calling on Allah through His beautiful names, then ask Him for whatever your heart longs for:

“Yā Allah, Al-Malik (The King), Al-Mālik (The Owner), Ar-Razzāq (the Giver of all provision), if anything were possible, I would ask You for…”
